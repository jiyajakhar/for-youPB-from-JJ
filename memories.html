Some things I notice... ❤️

Maybe I don't always say these things, but I notice them.

Your efforts

I know relationships aren't always easy. We've had our difficult days, our arguments, our misunderstandings and moments when things felt heavy.

But behind all of that, I have also seen you trying.

The difficult days

We've had days where we didn't understand each other properly.

Days where a small thing became a big fight. Days where both of us were tired, hurt or frustrated.

But somehow, we kept coming back and trying to understand each other again.

And the little things...

The random conversations. The silly moments. The laughter. The comfort. The feeling of having someone who knows you a little more than everyone else.

So...

After everything, I just wanted to make something that reminds you that

your efforts are seen. ❤️
